I am a Ph.D. candidate at Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Renjie Wan. My research interests lie in neuromorphic vision, AI security, digital watermarking,change detection, and related areas. I am passionate about building trustworthy and robust vision systems.

  • Watermarking & Forensics — robust invisible watermarking, multimedia forensics, data protection
